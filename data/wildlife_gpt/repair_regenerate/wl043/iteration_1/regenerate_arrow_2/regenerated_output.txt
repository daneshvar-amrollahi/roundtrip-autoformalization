If a person sells an animal, the sale must be only to a zoo entity, or by a zoo entity, or to an educational institution or research institution that is acting for a scientific purpose and at that time has a permit and subchapter authorization, unless the buyer at that time holds a commercial fishing license and, at some later time, sells that same animal to a zoo entity or to an educational institution or research institution that at the time of resale is acting for a scientific purpose and has a permit and subchapter authorization.
