A person may sell an animal only when the sale is to or from a zoo entity, or when the sale is to an educational institution or research institution that is acquiring the animal for a scientific purpose and has a permit at the time of the sale, or when the sale is to a dealer who buys the animal for a commercial purpose and who later sells the animal only to or from a zoo entity or to an educational institution or research institution that is acquiring the animal for a scientific purpose and has a permit at the time of that later sale.
