If a person possesses a fish, game fish, nongame fish, finfish, or shark that is named in the protected fish list, and the person has more than three times the possession limit for that kind at that time, and the person possesses at least one such protected fish of that kind, and every such protected fish of that kind the person possesses at that time lacks lawful documentation, then the person must possess at least one such protected fish of that kind at that time without lawful documentation.
