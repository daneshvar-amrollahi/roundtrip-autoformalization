If a person possesses a seine, strike net, gill net, trammel net, trotline, hook and line, crab trap, or other fishing device while on public water, and that device is not permitted by this code at that time, then the person must not be inside a powerboat, sailboat, floating device, or other vessel on coastal public water with that device on the vessel while the person is leaving that coastal public water or while the person is on public water where the device is permitted by this code.
