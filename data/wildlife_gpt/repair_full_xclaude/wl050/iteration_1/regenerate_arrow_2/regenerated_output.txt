If a person possesses a seine, strike net, gill net, trammel net, trotline, hook and line, crab trap, or other fishing device while on a public water body and that device is not permitted by this code at that time, then the person may do so only if the device is on a powerboat, sailboat, floating device, or other vessel and the person is on a water body that is both coastal water and public water, and either the person is on some land of an “other” land type or, while the person is on a canal or artificial waterway on that coastal public water, the device is permitted by this code at that time and there exists a public water body.
