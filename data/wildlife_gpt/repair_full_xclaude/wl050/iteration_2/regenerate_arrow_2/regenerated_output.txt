If a person possesses on a public water body any fishing device that is an other fishing device, seine, strike net, gill net, trammel net, trotline, hook and line, or crab trap and that device is not permitted by this code, then the person may do so only if the person is inside a powerboat, sailboat, floating device, or other vessel on coastal water and either the person is also on some land, or the person is on a canal or artificial waterway and the device is permitted by this code on a public water body.
