A person commits an offense if the person: (1) fails to immediately destroy and discard a shark fin as required by Subsection (b) or otherwise violates that subsection; (2) buys or offers to buy, sells or offers to sell, possesses for the purpose of sale, transports or ships for the purpose of sale, or advertises for sale a shark fin regardless of where the shark was taken or caught; or (3) violates a proclamation or rule adopted under this section.
