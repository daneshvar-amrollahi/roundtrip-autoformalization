An offense occurs if, at any time, a person possesses a fin that is a fin and is part of a shark, and either the fin has not been destroyed or the person has not lawfully disposed of that fin; or if, at any time, a person buys, offers to buy, sells, offers to sell, advertises for sale, possesses for sale, transports for sale, or ships for sale a fin that is a fin and is part of a shark; and there is also an additional offense condition stated as false, meaning no offense can ever occur under that third stated condition.
