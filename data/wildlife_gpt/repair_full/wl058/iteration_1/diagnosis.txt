First Failed Arrow: 1

Reasoning:
The Phase 1 SMT does not match the Original NL because the NL says an offense occurs when a person leaves edible fish or bait fish taken from public waters to die **without the intent to retain the fish for consumption or bait** (i.e., the required condition is the *absence* of intent to retain for those purposes). In contrast, Phase 1 encodes the conclusion as `not (and (has_intent_to_retain ...) (or (for_home_destination ...) (is_in_edible_condition ...)))`, which only forbids the *combination* of having intent to retain together with either being for home destination or being in edible condition; this allows cases where the person has intent to retain but the fish is not for home destination and not in edible condition, and it also introduces “home destination/edible condition” as a proxy for “consumption or bait” rather than directly encoding “for consumption or bait.”
