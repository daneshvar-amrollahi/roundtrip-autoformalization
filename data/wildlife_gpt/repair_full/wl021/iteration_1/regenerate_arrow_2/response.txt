If a written consent document is a valid consent from a landowner to a grantee at a given time, then the document must name the grantee, must identify some land, and must be signed by a person whose address and phone number are shown on the document and who is either the landowner, the landowner’s agent, a person who has the landowner’s consent at that time, or a person for whom the same landowner has given valid consent in that document at that time.
