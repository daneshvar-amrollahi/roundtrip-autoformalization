A person commits an offense if the person possesses a fish described by Subsection 66.020(a) of this code under Subsection 66.020(b) of this code and: (1) fails to keep with the fish a document or documents that verify the place of origin of the fish; or (2) fails, on the request of a game warden, to present to the game warden without delay a document or documents that verify the place of origin of the fish.
