Except as provided by Subsection (b), a person may not hunt or take any wild animal or wild bird when the person is on or over privately owned land that is: (1) submerged under: (A) public fresh water due to seasonal or occasional innundation; or (B) public salt water and located above the mean high tide line of the Gulf of Mexico and its bays and estuaries; and (2) conspicuously marked as privately owned by a sign or signs that are substantially similar to the following: POSTED. PRIVATE PROPERTY. NO HUNTING.
