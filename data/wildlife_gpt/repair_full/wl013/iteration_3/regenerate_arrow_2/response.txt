If a person hunts or takes any wild bird, wild game bird, wild fowl, wild game animal, game animal, game bird, nongame animal, reptile, amphibian, bat, wolf, armadillo, dangerous wild animal, deer, mule deer, white-tailed deer, desert bighorn sheep, pronghorn antelope, alligator, frog, turtle, exotic animal, exotic livestock, or exotic fowl on or over private land that is conspicuously marked as private and that is submerged under public fresh water, or submerged under public salt water above mean high tide in the Gulf of Mexico or a bay or estuary, then the person must not hunt or take that animal unless the person has subchapter authorization at that time.
