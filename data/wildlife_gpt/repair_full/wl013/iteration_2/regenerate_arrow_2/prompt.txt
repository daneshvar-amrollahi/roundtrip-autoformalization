Your task is to translate the SMT formula back into clear, natural language that describes the wildlife rule.

TRANSLATION REQUIREMENTS:
- Write as a single-sentence rule (or two sentences if complex)
- Use modal verbs: "must", "may not", "shall", "must not", "no person may"
- Use conditional structure: "If... then...", "unless", "when", "while"

- Do NOT use mathematical notation or logical symbols

CRITICAL SEMANTIC PRESERVATION:
1. MODAL VERBS - Preserve exact quantifier meaning:
   - Existential (∃) → "may" / "is allowed to" / "can"
   - Universal with negation (∀...¬) → "must not" / "shall not" / "may not" / "no person may"
   - Universal affirmative (∀...) → "must" / "shall" / "is required to"

2. DO NOT ADD CONDITIONS that aren't in the formula:
   - If formula has (¬A ∨ B), translate as "not A unless B"
   - Do NOT add extra conditions like "or C" if C isn't in the formula
   - Example: (or (not on_road_or_right_of_way) (during_open_season AK_Reptile))
     ✓ CORRECT: "may not be on the road or right-of-way unless it is during the reptile open season"
     ✗ WRONG: "may not be on the road or right-of-way unless during the reptile open season or hunting season"

3. DO NOT REMOVE CONDITIONS that are in the formula:
   - Every predicate in the formula MUST appear in the natural language
   - Don't try to "simplify" by omitting technical details
   - ESPECIALLY preserve:
     * Type constraints: (is_kind a AK_Bat) → mention "bat"
     * Context predicates: (on_water_body p w t) → mention "on a water body"
     * Existence checks: (has_document_kind p DOK_HuntingLicense t) → mention "with a hunting license"
     * Distinctness: (distinct p owner) → mention "another person" / "the landowner" (not just "a person")

4. PRESERVE EXCEPTION CONDITIONS:
   - If formula has (and ... (not X)), the exception X MUST appear in natural language
   - Example: (=> (and (during_night t) (not (during_regular_hunting_hours t))) (not (hunts p a t)))
     ✓ CORRECT: "may not hunt at night except during regular hunting hours"
     ✗ WRONG: "may not hunt at night" ← exception lost!
   - Common pattern: (=> (and A (not exception)) prohibition) → "prohibited unless exception"

5. PRESERVE TEMPORAL STRUCTURE:
   - Nested quantifiers (∀ t. ∀ t2. (<= t t2) ...) → "once started, for all future times" / "until"
   - Single quantifier (∀ t. ...) → "at any time when" / "while" / "if"
   - Existential (∃ t. ...) → "at some point" / "eventually"

6. TRANSLATE LITERALLY - Do NOT interpret or embellish:
   - Translate what the formula says, not what you think it should say
   - Don't add context or explanation that isn't in the formula

GRANULARITY BALANCE:
- NOT too technical: Avoid "for all persons p and animals a and times t such that..."
- NOT too vague: Avoid "people should respect wildlife" or "follow hunting rules"
- JUST RIGHT: Clear, specific conditions and obligations similar to written wildlife law

- NO markdown formatting
- NO labels like "Rule:" or "Translation:"
- Just the rule text itself

SMT-LIB FORMULA:
(forall ((hunter Person) (a Animal) (l Land) (t Int))
  (=> (and (or (hunts hunter a t) (takes hunter a t))
           (or (protected_by_code a) (is_kind a AK_WildBird))
           (or (on_land hunter l t) (over_land hunter l t))
           (is_private_land l)
           (or (submerged_under l WK_PublicFreshWater t)
               (and (submerged_under l WK_PublicSaltWater t)
                    (above_mean_high_tide l)))
           (conspicuously_marked_as_private l)
           (not (authorized_by_commission hunter t)))
      (not (or (hunts hunter a t) (takes hunter a t)))))
