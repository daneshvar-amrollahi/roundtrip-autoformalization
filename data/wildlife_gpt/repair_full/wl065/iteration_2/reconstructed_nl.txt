If a person receives from another state, imports into this state, transports, or sells a Micropterus bass, blue marlin, crappie, flathead catfish, jewfish, longbill spearfish, muskellunge, northern pike, red drum, sailfish, sauger, snook, spotted sea trout, striped bass, tarpon, walleye, white bass, white marlin, or yellow bass, or any hybrid fish that includes any of those fish as a part, then the person must have a finfish import license, and it is unlawful to do so without that license.
