DIAGNOSIS

First Failed Arrow: 1

Reasoning:
The Original NL prohibits entering or remaining on **public land** or on **private land without the landowner’s or his agent’s consent** when the person intends to disrupt a lawful hunter/catcher; this means the private-land condition is satisfied if the person lacks consent from **either** the landowner **or** the agent (i.e., no consent from the relevant authorizer). Phase 1 SMT instead makes the private-land condition require, for every landowner of the land, both (i) no consent from that owner and (ii) for every agent of that owner, no consent from that agent—so it only triggers when the person lacks consent from **the owner and all agents simultaneously**. That is stricter than the NL (it allows cases where the person has consent from an agent but not the owner, or vice versa), so Phase 1 does not correctly encode the Original NL.
