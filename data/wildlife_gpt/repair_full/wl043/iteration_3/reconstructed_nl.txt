If a person sells an animal to another person and that other person buys the animal at that time, then the sale and purchase may not occur unless either the seller is a zoo entity or the buyer is a zoo entity, or the buyer is an educational institution or research institution acting for a scientific purpose and has subchapter authorization at that time, or after that time the buyer later sells the same animal and the later transaction is to a zoo entity or to an educational institution or research institution acting for a scientific purpose with subchapter authorization at the time of that later sale.
