If a person sells an animal, the sale must be to a buyer who buys that animal at that time and who is a zoo entity, or the seller must be a zoo entity, or the buyer must be an educational institution or a research institution acting for a scientific purpose and having subchapter authorization at that time, unless the animal is sold to a dealer who buys it at that time and later sells it to another buyer who buys it at that later time and who is a zoo entity, or the dealer is a zoo entity, or that later buyer is an educational institution or a research institution acting for a scientific purpose and having subchapter authorization at that later time.
