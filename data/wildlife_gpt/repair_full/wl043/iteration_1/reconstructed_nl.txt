No person may sell an animal unless the seller or the buyer is a zoo entity, or the buyer is an educational institution or research institution that is acquiring the animal for a scientific purpose and has subchapter authorization at the time of purchase, or the buyer is a dealer purchasing the animal for a commercial purpose and, at some time on or after that sale, the dealer sells the animal only to a zoo entity or to an educational institution or research institution that is acquiring it for a scientific purpose and has subchapter authorization at the time of that later sale.
