If a person possesses or uses a seine, strike net, gill net, or trammel net while on a public water body, then the net must have float spacing of six feet or less and must have the required buoyancy. A person may not possess a seine, strike net, gill net, or trammel net on navigation district waters unless the net is permitted by this code, and no person may fish for any fish while possessing such a net on navigation district waters when the net is not permitted by this code.
