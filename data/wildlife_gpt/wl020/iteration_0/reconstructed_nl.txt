If a person who is not the landowner is present on the landowner’s land, possesses a firearm, and is hunting or engaging in target shooting, and the animal involved is a wild bird, a wild game animal, a nongame animal, a wolf, a bat, an armadillo, a dangerous wild animal, a deer, a mule deer, a white-tailed deer, a desert bighorn sheep, a pronghorn antelope, an alligator, a reptile, or an amphibian, then the person must not hunt that animal and must not engage in target shooting unless the person has the landowner’s written consent.
