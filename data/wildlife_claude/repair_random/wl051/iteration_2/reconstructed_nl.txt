A person who catches, attempts to catch, or fishes for finfish for commercial purposes on public water while possessing or using a seine, strike net, gill net, or trammel net must ensure that the net has float spacing of no more than six feet and meets required buoyancy standards. A person who possesses a seine, strike net, gill net, or trammel net on coastal water within one-half mile of the water, where the net is not permitted by code, must be actively fishing for fish on that coastal water body.
