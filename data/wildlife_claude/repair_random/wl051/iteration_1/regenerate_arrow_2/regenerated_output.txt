A person who is on public water and catches, attempts to catch, or fishes for finfish, or possesses or uses a seine, strike net, gill net, or trammel net, must ensure that the net has a float spacing of no more than six feet and has the required buoyancy. A person may not possess a seine, strike net, gill net, or trammel net on or within one-half mile of public coastal water unless the device is permitted by code.
