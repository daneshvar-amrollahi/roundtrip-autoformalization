A person may not hunt any wild bird, wild game bird, wild fowl, wild game animal, game animal, game bird, nongame animal, reptile, amphibian, bat, wolf, armadillo, dangerous wild animal, deer, mule deer, white-tailed deer, desert bighorn sheep, or pronghorn antelope—other than an alligator, frog, or turtle—while using and riding inside an aircraft, airborne device, motor vehicle, powerboat, sailboat, or other floating device, unless that person has authorization under the applicable subchapter.
