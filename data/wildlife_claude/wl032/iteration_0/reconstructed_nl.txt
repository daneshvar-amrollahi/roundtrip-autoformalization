A person may not import into this state any wild bird, wild game bird, wild fowl, game bird, wild game animal, or game animal that is protected by this code if the animal is intended for sale, barter, exchange, or shipment for sale, and the animal is of a kind for which the open season is in effect, and the person is importing the animal for sale, barter, or exchange or during the open season for that kind of animal, unless the person has authorization under the applicable subchapter.
