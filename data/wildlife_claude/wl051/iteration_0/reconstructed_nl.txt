A person who is fishing for finfish on public water while possessing or using a seine, strike net, gill net, or trammel net must ensure that the net has float spacing of no more than six feet and has the required buoyancy. A person who possesses a seine, strike net, gill net, or trammel net that is not permitted by code while on public coastal water or within half a mile of such water may do so only if that person is at some time lawfully engaged in fishing.
