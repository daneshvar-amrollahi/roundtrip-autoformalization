No person may possess a device designed to catch fish or other aquatic wildlife in or on the public water of this state where the use of the device is not permitted by this code or by a proclamation of the commission under this code unless the device is on board a vessel that is in public coastal water and is: (1) in port; or (2) in a marked channel and the vessel is going directly to or from public water in this state where the use of the device is permitted.
