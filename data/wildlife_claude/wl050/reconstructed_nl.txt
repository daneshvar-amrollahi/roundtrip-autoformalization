A person who possesses a seine, strike net, gill net, trammel net, trotline, hook and line, crab trap, or other fishing device on public water, where such device is not otherwise permitted by code, must have that device on board a powerboat, sailboat, or other vessel while on coastal water, and either an animal must be inside or on a building or the person must be on a canal or artificial waterway with the device permitted by code.
