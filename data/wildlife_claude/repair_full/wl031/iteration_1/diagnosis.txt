Reasoning:
The Phase 3 SMT encoding incorrectly decouples the species membership check from the closed season check. The Reconstructed NL states the prohibition applies "during the closed season for that species," meaning the closed season must be for the same species the animal belongs to. However, Phase 3 separates these into two independent conditions: (1) a disjunction checking `(is_kind a AK_X)` for the listed kinds, and (2) an existential `(exists ((ak AnimalKind)) (and (is_kind a ak) (during_closed_season ak t)))` that could match a *different* animal kind than the one satisfying the disjunction. This means an animal could be classified as, e.g., AK_WildBird (satisfying the disjunction) while the existential is satisfied by some other kind's closed season. The correct encoding should tie the closed season check to the same specific kind, as Phase 1 does with paired conjunctions like `(and (is_kind a AK_WildBird) (during_closed_season AK_WildBird t))`. Additionally, Phase 3 places `imports_to_state` in both the antecedent and consequent, which, while logically reducible, is structurally different from the Reconstructed NL's straightforward prohibition.
