This section does not apply to a person who sells an animal if any of the following conditions is satisfied: the person is a zoo entity or the buyer is a zoo entity; the buyer is an educational institution or research institution that is buying for a scientific purpose and holds a permit and has subchapter authorization; or the buyer is purchasing for commercial purposes and at a later time resells the animal to either a zoo entity or to an educational or research institution that is buying for a scientific purpose and holds a permit and has subchapter authorization at that later time.
