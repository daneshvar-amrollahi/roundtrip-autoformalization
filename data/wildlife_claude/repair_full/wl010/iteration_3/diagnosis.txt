Phase 3 SMT incorrectly encodes the Reconstructed NL in its conclusion. The Reconstructed NL states that the person "may use a laser sighting device," which should mean the hunter uses the specific laser sighting device `d` that is universally quantified in the formula's scope (as in: for any laser sighting device, the hunter may use it). Instead, Phase 3 introduces an existential `(exists ((d2 Device)) (and (device_kind d2 DK_LaserSightingDevice) (uses_device hunter d2 t)))` in the consequent while keeping `d` constrained as a laser device in the antecedent but never referencing `d` in the consequent. This makes `d` vacuously universally quantified in the antecedent (the formula's truth depends on whether any laser device exists to bind `d`), and the conclusion only asserts existence of some laser device being used rather than the specific device `d` being used. The consequent should be `(uses_device hunter d t)` to match the universal quantification pattern and the Reconstructed NL's straightforward permission to use a laser sighting device.
