The Phase 1 SMT encoding incorrectly handles clause (2) of the Original NL. The Original NL states that no person may sell or offer for sale a part of a dangerous wild animal that is used in a controlled kill — this prohibits **any** person from selling parts of such an animal, regardless of who killed it. However, the Phase 1 SMT encoding includes `(kills p a t)` in the antecedent of the part-selling prohibition, which restricts the prohibition to only the same person `p` who killed the animal. The quantified variable `p` is shared between the killing condition and the selling prohibition, meaning only the killer is barred from selling parts. The correct encoding should allow for any person to be prohibited from selling parts of an animal that was killed in a controlled kill, potentially using a separate existential quantifier for the killer or simply requiring that the animal was killed in such a context without binding the killer to the seller.
