First Failed Arrow: 3

Reasoning:
The Reconstructed NL states that no person may sell or offer to sell any part of a dangerous wild animal "that was killed after being released for killing," where the killing could have been performed by any person. However, Phase 3 SMT encodes the killing predicate as `(kills p a t_kill)` where `p` is bound to the same universally quantified variable representing the person who sells or offers to sell the part. This incorrectly restricts the prohibition to only cases where the seller is also the killer. The correct encoding should use an existentially quantified separate variable for the killer (e.g., `(exists ((killer Person)) (kills killer a t_kill))`) to capture that the animal was killed by anyone after being released for killing, not necessarily by the person attempting to sell the part.
