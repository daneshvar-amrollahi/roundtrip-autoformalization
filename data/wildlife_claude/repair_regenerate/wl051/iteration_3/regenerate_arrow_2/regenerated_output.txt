A person who is on any public freshwater, public saltwater, public water, coastal water, Gulf of Mexico, or bay or estuary and who possesses or uses a seine, strike net, gill net, or trammel net to fish for, catch, or attempt to catch finfish must ensure that the net has float spacing of no more than six feet and meets required buoyancy standards. A person who possesses a seine, strike net, gill net, or trammel net on or within one-half mile of coastal water, unless the net is permitted by code, may do so only if the person is fishing for fish on that coastal water.
