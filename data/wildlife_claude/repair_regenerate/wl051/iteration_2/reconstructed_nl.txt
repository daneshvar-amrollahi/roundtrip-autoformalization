A person who is fishing for finfish for commercial purposes on public water using a seine, strike net, gill net, or trammel net must ensure that the device has float spacing of no more than six feet and meets required buoyancy standards. A person who possesses a seine, strike net, gill net, or trammel net on public coastal water within one-half mile of the water and the device is not permitted by code shall not possess such device unless the person is actively fishing on a water body with a device that is permitted by code.
