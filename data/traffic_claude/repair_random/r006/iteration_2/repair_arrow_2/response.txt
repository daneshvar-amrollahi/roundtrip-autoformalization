An operator driving on a two-way roadway with four or more lanes must not drive on the left half of the roadway, unless the operator is in an authorized lane, or unless an obstruction is present and the operator is yielding the right-of-way to a pedestrian, or unless the operator is crossing the center line while either intending to turn left or turning into or emerging from an alley, private road, or driveway located on that roadway.
