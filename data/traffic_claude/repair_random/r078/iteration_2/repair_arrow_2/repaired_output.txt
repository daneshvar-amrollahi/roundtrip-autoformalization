If an operator is on a roadway where an authorized emergency vehicle is also present and stationary with its emergency signals active, and the operator has not vacated the lane nearest to the emergency vehicle, then the operator must reduce speed: if the posted speed limit on that roadway is less than 25 miles per hour, the operator must not exceed 5 miles per hour; otherwise, the operator must not exceed a speed equal to the posted speed limit minus 20 miles per hour.
