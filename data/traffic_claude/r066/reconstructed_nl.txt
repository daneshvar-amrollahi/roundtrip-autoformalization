When an operator is approaching an uncontrolled intersection on a roadway that has one or two lanes, and another vehicle on a different roadway entering the same intersection is on a divided roadway or a roadway with marked lanes numbering three or more, and that other vehicle is either in the intersection or approaching it and constitutes an immediate hazard, the operator must stop, yield the right-of-way to pedestrians, and ensure there is sufficient space for the other vehicle to enter.
