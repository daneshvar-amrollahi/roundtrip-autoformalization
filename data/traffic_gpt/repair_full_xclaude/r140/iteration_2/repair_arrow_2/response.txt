If an operator is passing another vehicle on the right on a roadway, and both vehicles are on that roadway at that time, and the roadway is of sufficient width or has marked lanes, then the requirement that the passed vehicle remain on the right half of the roadway from that time onward while it has not yet been completely passed and an audible signal is being given does not apply, and the requirement that the passed vehicle not accelerate from that time onward while it has not yet been completely passed does not apply.
