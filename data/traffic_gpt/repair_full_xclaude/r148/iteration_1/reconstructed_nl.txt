If a vehicle is on a roadway and it is not in a condition where it is standing without being parked or parked without being standing, then the operator must not stop or park the vehicle when it is within 20 feet of a fire station driveway, or, on a two-way roadway, when it is on the left half of the roadway or in the left lane or center lane and within 75 feet of a fire station driveway.
