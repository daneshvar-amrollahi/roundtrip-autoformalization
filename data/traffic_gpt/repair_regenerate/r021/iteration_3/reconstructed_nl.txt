If an operator is passing another vehicle on the right on a roadway, then the operator must ensure the movement can be made safely, the other vehicle must be intending to turn left or executing a left turn, and the pass must occur either on a roadway with marked lanes and at least two lanes, or on an unmarked roadway that is of sufficient width, or from the right shoulder only when that shoulder use is necessary and safe and is for passing a vehicle that is slowing, stopped, disabled, or turning left.
