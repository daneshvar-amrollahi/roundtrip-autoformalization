If two different vehicles are traveling on the same one-way roadway that has at least three lanes, with each vehicle in a lane adjacent to the same target lane, and one vehicle intends to change lanes left into that target lane while the other intends to change lanes right into that target lane, and each lane change can be made safely with sufficient space for each vehicle to enter without conflict, then the operator of the vehicle changing left must yield the right-of-way to a pedestrian at that time.
