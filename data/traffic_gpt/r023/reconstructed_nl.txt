If a vehicle is on a roadway and is driving on the right shoulder, then the operator must do so only when that shoulder use is necessary and safe, and only for stopping, standing, or parking, or for accelerating before entering the main lane, or for decelerating before making a right turn, or for passing a vehicle that is slowing, stopped, disabled, or turning left, or for allowing a faster vehicle to pass, or to comply with a traffic control device, or to avoid a collision.
