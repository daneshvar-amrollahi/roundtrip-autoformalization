If an operator is on a roadway and approaching an uncontrolled intersection on that roadway, and the roadway has one or two lanes and is either divided or has marked lanes with at least three lanes, and another vehicle is approaching from the opposite direction and is in the intersection or also approaching the intersection and is an immediate hazard to the operator, then the operator must stop, must yield the right-of-way to a pedestrian, and must not enter the intersection.
