If a vehicle is on a paved roadway and is driving on the right shoulder, then the operator must use the shoulder only when that use is necessary and safe, and only for stopping, standing, or parking, for accelerating before entering the main lane, for decelerating before making a right turn, for passing a slowing, stopped, disabled, or left-turning vehicle, for allowing a faster vehicle to pass, to comply with a traffic-control device, or to avoid a collision.
