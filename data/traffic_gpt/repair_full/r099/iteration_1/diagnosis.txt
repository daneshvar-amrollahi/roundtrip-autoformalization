First Failed Arrow: 1

Reasoning:
The Phase 1 SMT does not encode a general prohibition on standing or parking in front of a driveway with an exception for momentary pickup/discharge; instead, it only forbids standing/parking in front of a driveway when it is **not** the case that there exists some later time the vehicle is not in front of the driveway and also exists some (possibly different) later time the vehicle is in front of the driveway again. This “must eventually leave and later be in front again” pattern is unrelated to the stated exception (momentarily to pick up or discharge a passenger) and also makes the prohibition depend on future occupancy of the driveway area rather than on whether the stop is momentary for passenger pickup/discharge.
