If an operator is driving on a roadway with a posted speed limit and is approached on that roadway by an emergency vehicle that is stationary with its emergency signals active, then the operator must not exceed a speed that is five miles per hour below the posted speed limit when the posted limit is less than twenty-five miles per hour, and must not exceed a speed that is twenty miles per hour below the posted speed limit when the posted limit is twenty-five miles per hour or more, while the operator has not vacated the lane nearest the emergency vehicle.
