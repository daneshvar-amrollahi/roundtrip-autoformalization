If a vehicle on a roadway with an uncontrolled intersection, where that roadway has one or two lanes and is either divided or has marked lanes with at least three lanes, is approaching the intersection and another vehicle on a different roadway is in or approaching the same intersection and poses an immediate hazard, then the operator must stop and must not enter or be in the intersection, and the operator must not proceed until that other vehicle is no longer an immediate hazard to the operator.
