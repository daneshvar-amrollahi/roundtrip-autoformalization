Reasoning:
The Phase 1 SMT does not correctly capture the Original NL because the NL condition is specifically “on a streetcar track in front of a streetcar” (i.e., the track is relevant to that streetcar), whereas the SMT only requires that `sc` is a streetcar, that `v` is on some roadway `r` with position `RP_StreetcarTrack`, and that `v` is in front of `sc`; it never asserts that `sc` is on the same roadway/track (or otherwise relates `sc` to `r`/the track). As a result, the SMT can trigger the obligation even when the vehicle is on a streetcar track but the referenced streetcar is unrelated to that track/roadway, which is not faithful to the original rule’s intended scenario.
