If a vehicle and another vehicle are on the same two-way roadway that is not of sufficient width and they are approaching each other from opposite directions, then the operator must keep to the right half of the roadway while the other vehicle keeps to the left half, or keep to the left half while the other vehicle keeps to the right half, unless it is not practicable for the operator’s vehicle to remain within a single lane and the operator has proceeded with due caution, or it is not practicable for the other vehicle to remain within a single lane and that operator has proceeded with due caution.
