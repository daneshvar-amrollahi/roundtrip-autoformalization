If a vehicle on an unpaved roadway is approaching an intersection with a paved roadway, and another vehicle on the paved roadway is in or approaching that intersection and is an immediate hazard, then the operator of the vehicle on the unpaved roadway must stop and must yield the right-of-way to a pedestrian, and the operator must not enter the intersection while the vehicle on the paved roadway remains in or approaching the intersection and continues to be an immediate hazard.
