If an operator’s vehicle and an authorized emergency vehicle are on the same roadway and the emergency vehicle is approaching the operator’s vehicle, the operator must, at that time, move as close as practicable to the right edge of the roadway, position the vehicle parallel to the curb, not be in any intersection on that roadway, and stop, and the operator must remain standing and stopped from that time onward at every time until the emergency vehicle has completely passed the operator’s vehicle.
