If a vehicle is on a roadway that has exactly three lanes, carries two-way traffic, and the vehicle is in the center lane at that time, then the vehicle may be in the center lane only if it is passing (with the left side clearly visible and free for passing and, at that time, at a safe distance from every other vehicle that is on that roadway), or it is intending to make a left turn, or it is doing so as directed by a traffic control device.
