If a vehicle on an unpaved roadway is approaching an intersection where that unpaved roadway intersects a paved roadway, and at that same time another vehicle on the paved roadway is in the intersection or approaching the intersection and is an immediate hazard to the vehicle on the unpaved roadway, then the operator of the vehicle on the unpaved roadway must stop, must yield the right-of-way to a pedestrian, and must not enter the intersection at that time.
