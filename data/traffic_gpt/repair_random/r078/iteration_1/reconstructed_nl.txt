If a vehicle is on a roadway with an authorized emergency vehicle that is stationary with its emergency signals active, and the vehicle has not vacated the lane nearest that emergency vehicle, and the roadway has a posted speed limit, then the operator must reduce speed as appropriate and must not drive faster than 5 miles per hour when the posted speed limit is less than 25 miles per hour, or faster than 20 miles per hour below the posted speed limit when the posted speed limit is 25 miles per hour or more.
