If a vehicle is on a roadway and is operating on the right shoulder, then the operator must use the shoulder only when that shoulder use is necessary and safe, and only for stopping, standing, or parking, for accelerating before entering the main lane, for decelerating before making a right turn, for passing a vehicle that is slowing, stopped, disabled, or turning left, for allowing a faster vehicle to pass, for complying with a traffic control device, or for avoiding a collision.
