An operator must not drive on the right shoulder of a roadway unless, at that time, using the shoulder is necessary and safe and the reason for using the shoulder is to stop, stand, or park, to accelerate before entering the main traveled lane, to decelerate before making a right turn, to pass a slowing, stopped, disabled, or left-turning vehicle, to allow a faster vehicle to pass, to comply with a traffic control device, or to avoid a collision.
