An operator must not drive on the right shoulder of a roadway unless, at that time, using the shoulder is necessary and safe and the reason for using the shoulder is to stop, stand, or park; to accelerate before entering the main traveled lane; to decelerate before making a right turn; to pass a vehicle that is slowing, stopped, disabled, or turning left; to allow a faster vehicle to pass; to comply with a traffic control device; or to avoid a collision.
