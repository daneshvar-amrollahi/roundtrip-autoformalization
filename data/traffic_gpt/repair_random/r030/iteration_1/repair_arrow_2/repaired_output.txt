If, on a one-way roadway with at least three lanes, two different vehicles are in the same target lane on that roadway, and there are two different lanes on that same roadway adjacent to the target lane such that one of those adjacent lanes is also adjacent to the other, and the first vehicle intends to change lanes to the left from one adjacent lane into the target lane while the second vehicle intends to change lanes to the right from the other adjacent lane into the target lane, and each lane change can be made safely, then the operator of the first vehicle must yield the right-of-way to a pedestrian at that time.
