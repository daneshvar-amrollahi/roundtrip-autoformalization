If an operator’s vehicle is on a roadway with one or two lanes and is approaching an uncontrolled intersection on that roadway, and the roadway is either a divided highway or has marked lanes with at least three lanes, and another vehicle is also on that same roadway and is either in that intersection or approaching it and is an immediate hazard to the operator’s vehicle, then the operator must be stopped, must yield the right-of-way to pedestrians, and must not enter the intersection at that time.
