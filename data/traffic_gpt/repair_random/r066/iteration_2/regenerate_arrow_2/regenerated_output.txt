If an operator is on a roadway with one or two lanes and is approaching an uncontrolled intersection on that roadway, and the roadway is either a divided highway or has marked lanes with at least three lanes, then the operator must stop, must yield the right-of-way to a pedestrian, and must not enter the intersection while another vehicle on the same roadway that is not the operator’s vehicle is in or approaching that intersection and is an immediate hazard to the operator.
